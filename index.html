Your plan, month by month

Example: the 3-month plan. Longer plans use the same system with more practice and more mocks.

Month 1 · Learn

Go topic by topic. A topic test opens as soon as that topic is done, so you test what you have studied.

Month 2 · Practise

Weekly sectional tests and monthly subject tests show your weak sections, with a part-syllabus mock every week.

Month 3 · Mock phase

Full-length mocks in the real exam pattern. Once a mock is open, take it any day you want. The number of mocks depends on your exam and plan.

✅ Quick photo & signature tips

Plain white or light background · face clearly visible, looking straight · no cap or dark glasses · sign in dark ink on plain paper, inside the exam's own signature style (capital letters are rejected by some exams). Keep the original files — you'll need them again for admit-card and document-verification stages.
